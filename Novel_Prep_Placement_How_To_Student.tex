\section{开始 / Getting started}

\step{1}{登录页点紫色按钮}{Click \textbf{Start Placement Test}. No account is required.}

\fig[0.30\textheight]{登录页 / Login}{01_login_card.png}{\enzh{点 \textbf{Start Placement Test}。若已开考：点 \textbf{Already started? Recover with your code}，凭恢复码返回本场考试。}{Click \textbf{Start Placement Test}. If you already began, use \textbf{Recover with your code} to return to the same sitting.}}

\step{2}{只打开顾问指定的试卷}{Open only the test your advisor named.}

\fig[0.36\textheight]{试卷目录 / Test catalog}{02_catalog_live.png}{\enzh{四个紫色按钮对应四套试卷。请只打开顾问指定的一套，不要自行改选更难的试卷。}{The four purple buttons are the four tests. Open only the one your advisor named. Do not choose a harder paper on your own.}}

\vspace{4pt}
\fig[0.12\textheight]{恢复入口 / Resume}{02b_recover_cta.png}{\enzh{此处不会新开试卷。中途离开后，点这里粘贴恢复码，返回原场考试。}{This does not start a new test. After leaving, tap here, paste your recovery code, and return to the same sitting.}}

\section{报名资料与恢复码 / Profile and recovery code}

\step{3}{填写资料，顾问姓名必须选对}{Complete the form. Select the correct advisor.}

\fig[0.50\textheight]{姓名、年级、校内数学课、顾问 / Name, grade, school math class, advisor}{03_start_fields.png}{\enzh{顾问请选 \textbf{Mia Hu}、\textbf{Jimmy Zheng}，或 Other 并填写全名。选错则该顾问无法查看本场试卷。确认后点 Begin。}{Select \textbf{Mia Hu}, \textbf{Jimmy Zheng}, or Other and type the full name. If the wrong advisor is selected, they will not see this test. Check the box, then Begin.}}

\step{4}{先抄下恢复码，再 Begin exam}{Copy the recovery code, then Begin exam.}

\fig[0.36\textheight]{恢复码（有效期 24 小时） / Recovery code (24 hours)}{04_recovery_ticket.png}{\enzh{请立即点 Copy code，抄到备忘录或发给家长，不要只留在本网页。请尽量当场完成。如遇特殊情况中途离开，凭此码返回续考；自开考起仅有效 \textbf{24 小时}，逾期未提交则删除。}{Select Copy code at once and keep it outside this browser. Please finish in one sitting. If you must leave, this code is the only way back. It is valid for \textbf{24 hours} from the start. Unsubmitted work is then deleted.}}

\section{考试进行中 / During the examination}

\step{5}{屏幕作答会自动保存}{On-screen answers are saved as you go.}

\enzh{倒计时不会因离开网页而暂停或重置。可用 Previous / Jump。Middle / Upper 全部完成后即可 Submit。}{The countdown does not pause or restart if you leave the page. Previous / Jump may be used. Middle / Upper: Submit when finished --- there is no paper page.}

\Needspace{16\baselineskip}
\enzh{\textbf{Enhanced 屏幕最后一题}显示 \textbf{50/50}（EM1）或 \textbf{55/55}（EM2）。请点 \textbf{Next} 或 \textbf{Save \& continue to paper}。此时请勿 Final Submit。}{\textbf{Enhanced, last on-screen question:} \textbf{50/50} (EM1) or \textbf{55/55} (EM2). Click \textbf{Next} or \textbf{Save \& continue to paper}. Do not Final Submit yet.}

\fig[0.40\textheight]{屏幕最后一题 / Last on-screen MCQ}{05d_last_mcq_tight.png}{\enzh{紫色按钮 \textbf{Save \& continue to paper} 进入纸考上传页。}{The purple button opens the paper-upload page.}}

\step{6}{Enhanced：纸上作答，上传一份 PDF}{Enhanced only: complete the paper packet and upload one PDF.}

\fig[0.48\textheight]{下载空白卷 $\rightarrow$ 上传一份 PDF $\rightarrow$ Save and finish}{06c_paper_upload.png}{\enzh{作图与大题请合并为 \textbf{一份} PDF（不超过 20 MB），放入 \textbf{Drop one PDF here}。先 Save and finish，再 Final Submit。未上传 PDF 请勿提交。不接受照片。}{Place graphing and free response in \textbf{one} PDF (20 MB or less) on \textbf{Drop one PDF here}. Save and finish, then Final Submit. Do not submit without the PDF. Photographs are not accepted.}}
