分班测评使用教程

How students take English and Math diagnostics, and how advisors review essays, paper work, and PDF reports.
学生如何完成英语/数学测评；老师如何批阅写作、纸质题并导出报告。

Start placement · 进入测评 Download PDF · 下载 PDF

1 · Students 学生

  1. Open the placement hub Go to Placement, choose English or Math, then the test your counselor assigned. 打开 Placement，选择英语或数学，再点顾问指定的测评。
  2. Complete the profile Name, grade, school, and advisor. Check the counselor confirmation box. 填写姓名、年级、学校、顾问，并勾选确认项。
  3. Save your recovery code On the Ready screen, copy the 24-hour code. Use Recover sitting if you switch devices. 在 Ready 页保存 24 小时恢复码；换设备时用 Recover sitting。
  4. Work through parts English: Reading → Language → Writing essay. Math: follow on-screen gates; Enhanced Math also needs a paper PDF upload. 英语：阅读→语言→写作。数学：按关卡推进；强化数学还需上传纸质卷 PDF。
  5. Submit test When every part is done, use Submit test in the header and confirm. Writing is graded later by your advisor. 全部完成后点击页眉 Submit test 并确认。作文由顾问稍后评分。

2 · Advisors & teachers 顾问老师

  • Sign in with your staff account, then open Admin → Placement testers.
    使用教务账号登录，进入 Admin → Placement testers。
  • Filter Math vs English on the roster.
    名单可按数学/英语筛选。
  • Open a student for section scores, writing snapshot, and item-by-item review.
    点开学生查看各部分分数、写作摘要与逐题详情。
  • Grade English writing on the last item (20-point rubric). Save all criterion rows.
    在最后一题按 20 分量表给写作打分并保存。
  • Export report.pdf for parents.
    下载 report.pdf 给家长。

3 · Tests at a glance 测评一览

  • English G7–8 — 32 items, ~55 min · 阅读+语言+写作
    32 题，约 55 分钟
  • English G9–11 — 41 items, ~65 min
    41 题，约 65 分钟
  • Middle Level Math — 100 fill-in, ~150 min
    100 填空，约 150 分钟
  • Enhanced Math I / II — MCQ + paper PDF upload
    选择题 + 纸质卷 PDF
  • Upper Algebra / Precalc — 85 MCQ, five gates
    85 选择题，五关结构

4 · Help 帮助

  • Essay missing in admin? Student must Submit test after writing (autosave merges on submit).
    后台看不到作文？请确认学生写作后已 Submit test。
  • SAT Workspace accounts are separate — see the student guide.
    SAT 练习账号与分班分开，见学生指南。
